01 — The Project

Bringing a retail brand into the digital marketplace.

Shop Poge is an online retail project for fashion, perfumes, and accessories. The project explores how a clear visual identity, organized product categories, and convenient shopping workflows can help customers discover products and interact with a retail business.

02 — Core Features

A shopping experience designed around customers.

The following areas describe the project's intended shopping experience. Their availability depends on implementation and testing in the working store.

01

Product Categories

Separate product discovery areas for fashion, perfumes, and accessories, helping customers navigate the store more easily.

02

Product Discovery

An organized catalogue intended to help shoppers browse products and find items that match their interests.

03

Shopping Workflow

A foundation for customer shopping journeys, including cart functionality and a structured path toward checkout.

04

Responsive Experience

A design approach focused on making the store usable across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.

05

Database Integration

PHP and MySQL provide the foundation for database-driven product information and shopping-related application data.

06

Payment Experience

A checkout experience can integrate payment processing when the required provider setup, verification, and testing are complete.

03 — Technology

A practical foundation for an online store.

The project draws on established web technologies to support its frontend, application logic, database, and deployment workflow.
