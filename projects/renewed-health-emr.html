01 — The Project

Organizing healthcare information through technology.

Renewed Health Medical Centre EMR is a healthcare software project focused on creating a structured digital environment for patient information and healthcare operations. The goal is to support more organized record management and provide a foundation for additional clinical and administrative workflows.

02 — Key Capabilities

Designed around essential healthcare workflows.

The project is organized around the following functional areas. Features should be considered available only when implemented and tested in the working application.

01

Patient Records

A structured approach to patient registration, identification, record retrieval, and maintaining patient information.

02

Clinical Workflows

A foundation for organizing clinical information and supporting authorized healthcare staff as they work with patient records.

03

Staff Access

A role-aware application structure intended to support appropriate access to authorized areas of the system.

04

Application Dashboard

A central interface for navigating the application's major modules and organizing day-to-day software workflows.

05

Database Integration

PHP and MySQL provide the foundation for structured application data and database-driven features.

06

Security Considerations

Authentication, authorization, secure sessions, and careful handling of sensitive health information are important development priorities.
